Private Friend-Group Discord Bot

Jeff Bot

A private, non-commercial League of Legends Discord bot for tracking match stats, timeline VOD reviews, squad synergy, and AI-assisted post-game coaching with friends.

Install Jeff Bot on Discord GitHub Repository

What It Does

Daily & Weekly Recaps

Automated Discord summaries tracking win rate, KDA, CS/min, vision score, damage share, LP progression, and squad awards.

Timeline VOD Coaching

Analyzes Match-v5 timelines (@10/@15 lane gold & CS diffs, objective timings, and item spikes) for structured post-game feedback.

Wins vs. Losses & Pool Audit

Compares winning vs. losing game habits, audits champion pool win rates by role, and tracks measurable weekly goals.

Squad Synergy & Debriefs

Detects completed group matches for combined post-game debriefs and tracks duo/squad win rates compared to solo play.

Live Match Overview

Checks which friends are currently in a live game and displays ranks, champion mastery, and matchup context after loading in.

Responsible API Caching

Caches immutable Match-v5 and timeline payloads locally in SQLite to avoid redundant requests and respect Riot rate limits.

Slash Commands

/stats player [days] [queue]Player stats, rank, LP delta, and top champions
/review player [game_ago]Match-v5 timeline VOD breakdown and coaching takeaways
/coach player [days]Wins-vs-losses stat split and personalized coaching tips
/pool player [days]Champion pool audit and role performance breakdown
/goal player [refresh]View or set a measurable weekly improvement goal
/synergy [days]Squad and duo win rates together vs. solo
/debriefCombined scoreboard for your squad's latest group match
/live • /scout playerActive game status and live matchup overview
/versus • /damage • /masteryHead-to-head comparison, weekly damage leaderboard, and top mastery
